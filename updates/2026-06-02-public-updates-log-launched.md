Most of what I ship is private — client work, internal AI tooling, advisory
output. The polished pieces become posts or papers. Most don't. This repo is
the synthesis layer: a short paragraph that says "yes, this happened" without
exposing the code.

jackgierlich.com will pull this at build time and render the feed on the home
desktop. One markdown file per update; frontmatter is the contract. No CMS, no
database, no analytics — just version-controlled prose someone can read in raw
form on GitHub.

The next entry replaces this one as the example. This one stays as a real
event.
